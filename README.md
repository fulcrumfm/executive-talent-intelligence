# Executive Talent Intelligence
### An evidence-based exploration of enterprise hiring and the modern job search

**Author:** Vaughn Berger  
**Status:** Season 1 Introduction has been published to this GitHub research archive; the remaining investigations, implementation and outcomes are pending. LinkedIn publication is not confirmed.  
**Research reference date:** November 3, 2026.

## Why this project exists
The modern job search raises an important business question: **How do major U.S. enterprises define, identify, validate, and prioritize people whose skills create business value?**

This public research project examines hiring economics, recruiter decisions, applicant tracking technologies, executive qualifications, and career communication. It then tests whether evidence-based approaches can improve the clarity and effectiveness of an executive candidate's applications. Its practical case study is the author's own executive career search. That personal motivation is explicit, not concealed.

The goal is to demonstrate a rigorous approach to problem-solving: frame a question, gather recent evidence, challenge assumptions, develop an intervention, implement it, and disclose what happened—including disappointing or inconclusive results.

## Readers
- **Enterprise leaders, HR executives, talent acquisition teams, and executive recruiters:** Research on hiring efficiency, organizational needs, talent assessment, and technology.
- **Professionals and job seekers:** Accessible, compassionate explanations of recruiting systems and practical, evidence-grounded career strategy, including professionals in mid- and later-career transitions.

## Article promise
Each public article will lead with a clear business or career challenge, communicate significant findings quickly, and use an abstract, keywords, readable sections, at least one sourced visualization, limitations, and citations. We target approximately five minutes of reading time. Future forecasts are explicitly labeled; historic evidence will never be disguised as current measurement.

## Evidence and outcomes
Sources are prioritized from the most recent 12-month publication window, with the underlying collection period documented independently. Company claims, technical documentation, independent research, inference, and original experiment results are different evidence types and will be identified as such.

The primary outcome of the personal case study is **qualified first interviews per verified application**, supported by recruiter response, application delivery, and role-fit measures. Interview improvement is a goal, not a promised finding. Personally identifiable correspondence and private application records are not published.

## AI and transparency
AI tools may assist with collection, organization, drafting, and analytical quality checks. The author is responsible for approving interpretation and publication. Sources and visualization data will be documented. This is an independent, personally motivated inquiry; it is not a university-sponsored study or an endorsement by cited organizations.

## Current status
As of November 3, 2026, this repository establishes the public project framework. Individual research reports, article manuscripts, charts, and experiment results will be published only after evidence and disclosure review.
