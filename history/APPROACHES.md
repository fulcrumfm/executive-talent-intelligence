# Approach and decision history

## Historical candidate strategies explored
1. Broad executive master résumé for several function families.
2. Separate Sales, Operations, and Executive résumés.
3. Potential Executive Sales, Executive Operations, and Business Operations / Transformation positioning.
4. Generic application and cover-letter workflow using an assisted application service.
5. Reverse-engineer employer hiring criteria and ATS capabilities **before** deciding on résumé formats.
6. Combine research publishing with a transparent case study and a career-focused companion series.

These were discussed approaches and hypotheses. Listing them does not imply validation or successful implementation.

## Current approach, November 3, 2026
Study current enterprise hiring and qualification evidence, inventory candidate accomplishments accurately, synthesize the findings independently, implement a selected document/application strategy, and report measured results.

## Decision discipline
Each significant decision should include date, alternatives, evidence, owner approval, expected effect, validation measure, and whether it was reversed. Previous approaches remain in the archive rather than disappearing after changes.
