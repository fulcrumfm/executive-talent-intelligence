# Visualization policy and archive

All articles require one or more meaningful visualizations based on verified numerical data.

## Minimum deliverables
- Rendered PNG/SVG suitable for reading on a mobile LinkedIn feed.
- Source-data CSV or similarly accessible structured data, where permitted.
- Short caption, units, definitions, source URL, observation period, and chart creation date.
- Accessible descriptive text summarizing the chart's evidence.
- Reproducible plotting instructions or code when appropriate.

## Integrity controls
Do not substitute illustrative data for measured findings without conspicuous labels. Show denominators and sample sizes; never crop axes to exaggerate differences without a clear reason and disclosure. Distinguish forecasts from observed history, employer case studies from representative statistics, and correlation from causality.
