# Public disclosure and publication policy

## Purpose
This is a public professional research portfolio and transparent personal job-search experiment. Its aim is to share useful, credible analysis—not to pose as an independent academic institution or covertly induce recruitment.

## Safe-to-publish material
Approved research summaries, original readable visualizations, cited public sources, sanitized methodology, high-level implementation principles, and aggregate outcome observations.

## Never publish
Credentials or tokens; private emails or contact records; unapproved résumés or application details; identifying employer communications; proprietary documents; private repositories, organization names, operational workflows, system identifiers, automation settings, internal prompts, or nonpublic metrics.

## Separation requirement
The public record stands alone. It must not reveal or link to restricted research operations or their hosting infrastructure. Review README content, hyperlinks, Git history, commit messages, pull requests, issues, charts, metadata, generated assets, and publication logs before publishing.

## Article review
Verify current source dates; confirm exact claims and numerical chart data; label forecasts, inference, and unpublished outcomes; remove internal references; confirm author approval and license rights; record publication URL only after publication.

## AI use
Disclose substantive AI assistance where appropriate. Maintain human responsibility for source verification and conclusions. Do not claim affiliation with Harvard Business School or another cited organization.

## Corrections
Preserve a corrections history and prominently correct material errors; do not erase failed hypotheses or fabricate results.
