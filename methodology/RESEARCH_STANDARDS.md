# Research standards
**Baseline date:** November 3, 2026.

## Core questions
How are U.S. enterprises measuring hiring efficiency and candidate value? How do documented recruiting systems process qualifications? Which executive skills are explicitly sought? What candidate evidence can help a person and an organization assess mutual fit?

## Source inclusion
1. Target sources published from November 3, 2025 through November 3, 2026, principally dated 2026.
2. Record the actual underlying measurement period: a recent publication can use older data.
3. Prioritize U.S. enterprise employer material, primary ATS documentation, substantial datasets with methodology, and named organizational cases. Do not automatically generalize a global statistic to the U.S.
4. Separate executive hiring from hourly/high-volume hiring; extrapolations require warnings.
5. Retain contradictory evidence, exclusions, missing data, and limitations.

## Evidence categories
- **E1:** Independently documented empirical research with clear population and methods.
- **E2:** Primary technical documentation of a feature or workflow (proves capability, not universal deployment).
- **E3:** Named employer or vendor-reported case study (measure may not be independently audited).
- **E4:** Industry survey with sampling and limitations disclosed.
- **H:** Analyst interpretation or untested hypothesis.
- **U:** Unverified, incomplete, or inaccessible source; not accepted for factual claims.

These are evidence descriptors, not numerical research-quality scores.

## Claim record
For each material finding record: claim_id, topic, exact assertion, source_title, publisher, URL, publication_date, data_period, geography, population, sample_size (if given), source_type, excerpt/location, limitations, contradiction_notes, retrieval_date, and status.

## Conduct
No invented applicant tracking algorithms, automated rejection rates, résumé match percentages, source citations, employer settings, or outcomes. Distinguish rules configured by an employer from product capabilities. Check both the claim and its precise applicability before translating it into a recommendation.

## Original experiment
Record denominator as **verified completed applications**, not clicked or requested submissions. Track role fit, résumé variant, channel, posting date, follow-up, response, first interview, and missing status. Avoid causal conclusions from small or nonrandom samples. Report failures and unknowns explicitly.

## Public release
Publish only accepted, sourced, disclosure-cleared research. A public report may omit private working data and should identify that limitation without exposing private records.
